======================================================================
TILTMOUSE - QUICK START GUIDE (2-STEP SETUP)
======================================================================

1. ON YOUR PHONE:
   Install 'TiltMouse_v2.apk' on your Android phone and open it.
   Hold the phone horizontally in your hands.

2. ON YOUR LAPTOP:
   Connect your phone to your laptop via USB cable with USB Debugging enabled.
   Double-click 'start_gateway.bat' (Windows) or run './start_gateway.sh' (Mac/Linux).

3. STEER & PLAY:
   Tap 'ZERO CALIBRATE' on your phone to center the cursor.
   Tilt phone left/right to move the mouse cursor across your entire laptop screen!
   Drag the 'Desktop Cursor Mapping' bar to set vertical cursor height.
   Tap 'ACTIVE [TAP TO PAUSE]' at any time to freeze the cursor.
======================================================================
